  PLACEMENT
  PLACMENT OF THE LOGOTYPE IS AN IMPORTANT PART OF OUR
  IDENTITY. TO BUILD A STRONG RECOGNIZABLE BRAND WE NEED TO
  BE CONSISTANT IN PLACEMENT AND USE.

  OUR SECONDARY OPTION IS PLACING THE LOGOTYPE RIGHT ALIGNED
  ù AGAIN, EITHER TOP RIGHT OR BOTTOM RIGHT.
